The team

The squad is co-ed and open to adults of any background who can play at a reasonable standard and commit to a weekly league night. Obsidian AC is competitive without being unpleasant: players are expected to work, and expected to behave.

Indoor league information

Format

Six-a-side indoor, running clock, rolling substitutions, walls in play.

Season

League dates are confirmed by the venue. Fixtures are published here and sent to the squad as soon as they are set.

Home venue

Clark County Indoor Sports Center, 16311 NE 15th St, Vancouver WA. Free parking, spectators welcome.

Fees

Season fee per player covering league registration, referees and venue. Confirm current amount with Coach Arnold.

    Kit and crest

    Obsidian black at home, white away, both cut with the blue shard trim. Bring both shirts to every fixture: the league can ask a side to change strip on the night.

    Kit notes

    What to bring, what you get

    • Home: black shirt and shorts with blue and white trim.
    • Away: white shirt and shorts with black and blue trim.
    • Back of shirt: squad number with OBSIDIAN AC above it.
    • Bring your own: indoor shoes, shin pads, black socks, and a full water bottle.

    Front-of-shirt space is open to a sponsor for the season.
