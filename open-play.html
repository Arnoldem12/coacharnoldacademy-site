Pickup and open play soccer in Vancouver, WA

New in town, back from a long break, or just want a game this week? You do not need a team and you do not need to sign up for a season. Here is where people actually play around Vancouver, Camas and Clark County — outdoors for free, and indoors for a few dollars.

Outdoor pickup

Free, casual, adult pickup. No fee, no roster, no commitment. Turn up, split sides and play. Which of the two fields is used can change week to week, so it is worth arriving a few minutes early and looking for the group.

Lit turf, used for the midday Tuesday and Thursday games when Harmony is booked. Park in the main lot and walk round to the fields.

They are community pickup, organised by the players themselves. We list them because people ask us every week where to play. Times drift, fields get booked and winter weather cancels things — treat this as a starting point rather than a guarantee, and if you show up to an empty field, try the other venue listed for that day.

Indoor open play

Two arenas in Vancouver run drop-in open play year round. Both cost a few dollars, both need you to register in person the first time, and both are the fastest way to get regular games through the wet months.

If it is your first time

Bring two shirts

One dark, one light. Sides get split on the spot and nobody wants to play shirts-and-skins in February.

Check your footwear

Firm-ground boots or turf shoes outdoors. Indoors, flats or turf shoes only — most arenas will not let you on in metal studs.

Turn up ten minutes early

That is when teams get sorted. Say hello, say it is your first time, and someone will put you on a side.

Any level is fine

Midday pickup is genuinely mixed: ex-college players next to people who have not played since school. Play at the level you have got.

Bring water and shin pads

Pickup is friendly but it is still football. Shin pads are cheap and your shins are not.

Cash or card for indoor

Outdoor pickup is free. Indoor sessions charge at the door and want you registered in person the first visit, so allow a few extra minutes.

Want more than a kickabout?

Pickup keeps you sharp, but it will not fix a first touch or a weak left foot on its own. If you want structure alongside the casual games, there are three ways in.
